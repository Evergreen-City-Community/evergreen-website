# Google Business Profile: set-up pack

This is what puts the church on the map and in the "church near me" box. It is free.
Whoever sets it up must be able to receive the verification (a video, a call or a postcard
to the address). **Do this first; verification can take days.**

Go to https://www.google.com/business and press "Manage now". Sign in with a church-owned
Google account (not a personal one), and add a second owner so it is never lost.

## Keep it alive
- A post every week ("This Sunday: ..."), and one for Christmas, Easter and the anniversary. Posts show on the Profile.
- **Reviews are the strongest local signal.** Ask the congregation, honestly and without pay or gifts (Google forbids that), to leave a review. Use the "Get more reviews" link in the Profile, put its QR on the Sunday slide, and reply to every review, kindly and by name.
- Never use a fake address, extra "locations" or a fake rating; that gets the Profile removed.

## Then copy it elsewhere
- **Bing Places** (https://www.bingplaces.com): choose "Import from Google". Bing powers DuckDuckGo and others.
- **Apple Business Connect** (https://businessconnect.apple.com): puts the church on Apple Maps, which every iPhone uses.
- Make the name, address and phone **identical** everywhere. Different spellings weaken trust.
