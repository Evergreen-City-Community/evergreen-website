# Links, listings and mentions (off-site SEO)

Google trusts a church more when other real places mention it, with the same name, address and
a link to the website. Quality matters far more than quantity. **Never buy links or use "SEO
packages" that promise hundreds; that gets sites penalised.**

## 1. The church's own accounts (week 1, free, high value)
Make each of these say **Evergreen City Community Church, Kazhakootam, Trivandrum** and link to https://evergreencitycommunity.com:
- YouTube channel: the "About" description and the links section; put the website link in the first line of every video description.
- Instagram: bio, and the link in the bio.
- Spotify show: the show description, and the website link in each episode description.
- Facebook page (create one if it does not exist).
- WhatsApp Business profile (name, address, website, hours).

## 2. Map and directory listings (weeks 1 to 3; copy the name, address and phone exactly)
Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect (see `google-business-profile.md`), then:
- Justdial and Sulekha (large Indian directories; a "Religious places > Church" listing)
- Mappls (MapmyIndia), India's own map, free to add a place
- OpenStreetMap (add the church as a place; many apps read it)
- Foursquare
- Any active **Kerala or Trivandrum church directory**, and Christian "find a church" directories (search "Trivandrum church directory"; use only live, tidy ones; skip spammy ones)

## 3. Real relationships (these are the links Google values most)
| Who | Ask | Why they would |
|---|---|---|
| **The NET (thenet.church)** | A link to ECC from its partner or church pages. ECC already links to The NET once the games return. | Same ministry family, and you are close to it. |
| Partner and friendly churches in Kerala | "Churches we love" / links page | Mutual: link to them too. |
| Christian groups at **Technopark** and the **University of Kerala campus at Kariavattom** | A mention or link on their pages or newsletter, or an invitation | They reach exactly the people searching "church near Technopark". |
| Local Christian media and bloggers | A short piece about the 4th anniversary | A real event with real news value. |
| Ministries whose events ECC hosts or joins | Link on the event page | Natural. |

### A simple message to send
> Hello, I'm [name] from Evergreen City Community Church in Kazhakootam, Trivandrum, an English-speaking, multicultural church that meets Sundays at 10:30 AM. We'd be glad to be listed on [their page] so newcomers to Trivandrum can find us, and we'd happily link to you in return. Our site is https://evergreencitycommunity.com. Thank you, and God bless.

## 4. Reviews
Honest Google reviews from members are worth more than any directory. Ask on Sundays (QR on the slide), never pay or gift for them, reply to every one.

## 5. Content that earns links by itself
- A page for each message: title, verse, short summary, the video or Spotify link. (Search engines reward a living site.)
- Christmas and Easter service pages in October and March ("Christmas service in Trivandrum" is searched heavily every December).
- Real testimonies, with permission.
- A short, honest guide: "What to expect at an English-speaking church in Trivandrum".

## What to avoid
Buying links, directories that want payment for "ranking", copying text from other churches, stuffing the name with keywords, fake reviews, hiding text. These cause penalties that are slow to undo.
